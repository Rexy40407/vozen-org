TEXT TO SPEECH

Join and leave voice

Voice membership is explicit unless the server enables auto-join. The bot does not silently move between channels.

COMMANDS

The direct session controls

CommandPublic behaviour
/joinJoins the voice channel you are currently in.
/leaveLeaves the current voice channel.
/shut-upStops talking now and clears the whole queue.
01 · ContextBe in the voice channel where you want Vozen to speak.
02 · ConnectRun /join.
03 · PlayUse /tts, auto-read or a voice game.
04 · StopUse /leave for the session or /shut-up for immediate silence.
05 · RecoverIf the bot disconnects, retry the direct command after checking permissions.
Premium 24/7 is a separate server entitlement.

The optional in-call setting keeps Vozen in an otherwise empty voice channel and brings it back after restarts. It is not required for ordinary sessions.
