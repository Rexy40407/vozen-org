PERSONAL CONFIGURATION

Save favourite voices

Favourites belong to the member who creates them. They do not change the server default or expose a server-wide catalogue.

COMMANDS

Save, inspect and remove

CommandPurpose
/voice favorite model:…Add a voice to your personal favourites.
/voice favoritesShow your saved favourites.
/voice unfavorite model:…Remove one saved voice.
/voice recentShow the ten recently used voices.
