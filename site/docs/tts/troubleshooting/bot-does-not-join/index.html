TROUBLESHOOTING

Vozen does not join

The join path needs a member in a voice channel and Connect permission for Vozen in that channel.

01 · ContextJoin the voice channel you want to use.
02 · CommandRun /join.
03 · CheckRead the response for the exact missing Connect or Speak permission.
04 · FixUpdate the role or channel overwrite.
05 · RetryRun /join again, then use /tts.
No voice context

The member who runs /join must be in a voice channel.

Connect missing

Grant Connect to Vozen in that voice channel.

Speak missing

Grant Speak or remove a conflicting channel overwrite.
